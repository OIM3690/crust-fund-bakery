# Crust Fund Bakery

## Client Brief

**Client:** Emily, who owns Crust Fund Bakery, a few streets from campus.

**Purpose:** someone deciding where to get breakfast opens this and walks in.

**Audience:** office workers and students nearby in the morning, and people
planning an event who are looking for catering.

**Key action:** come to the shop. Second: ask about catering.

**Pages needed:** the weekly menu, hours and location, catering.

**Content status:** hours and address I have. The menu changes every week and
lives on Instagram as a photo, so she has to send the text each week. No usable
photos of the shop or the food yet. The logo is a phone picture of the sign.

**Style preferences:** she likes one local coffee shop's site and cannot say
why, so that is mine to work out.

**Later:** online ordering with payment. A static site cannot take money, and
she can live with a phone number until it exists.
