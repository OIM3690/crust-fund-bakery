# Website Project Proposal

## Crust Fund Bakery

### 1. Project Overview

The goal of this project is to create a simple, modern website for **Crust Fund Bakery**, a small bakery located near a college campus.

The website will focus on two main goals:

1. Encourage nearby students and office workers to visit the bakery.
2. Make it possible for customers to place catering and food orders online.

### 2. Target Audience

The primary audiences are:

* **College students** — looking for a quick breakfast before or between classes.
* **Office workers** — looking for a convenient breakfast option on their way to work.
* **Event customers** — people interested in ordering catering for events.

### 3. Proposed Website Sections

* **Home** — introduce the bakery and encourage visitors to stop by.
* **Menu** — display the current weekly menu.
* **Order Online** — allow customers to place and pay for orders online.
* **Catering** — explain the catering service and provide a way to request an order.
* **About** — provide basic information about Crust Fund Bakery.
* **Visit Us** — show location, hours, and other information customers need before visiting.
* **Instagram** — connect customers to the bakery's Instagram, where the weekly menu is posted.

### 4. Key Features

* Weekly menu that can be updated easily.
* Clear opening hours and location information.
* Online ordering and payment.
* Catering information and ordering/request functionality.
* Instagram integration.
* Mobile-friendly design for students and workers checking the site from their phones.
* Prominent calls-to-action such as **Order Online**, **View Menu**, and **Visit Us**.

### 5. Design Direction

The design should feel **modern, welcoming, and easy to use** without being overly complicated.

Since the bakery currently does not have professional food or shop photography, the initial design should work well with limited imagery. The existing logo can also be incorporated, with the possibility of replacing it with a higher-quality version later.

### 6. Content & Updates

Because the menu changes every week, the website should make updating the menu straightforward. The weekly Instagram menu can also remain an important source of current menu information.

### 7. Expected Outcome

The finished website should give customers the information they need quickly and make the next step obvious—**visiting the bakery, ordering food, or contacting the bakery about catering**.

### 8. Future Considerations

The website can be designed so additional functionality can be added later, such as more advanced ordering, promotions, customer accounts, or other online ordering features.
