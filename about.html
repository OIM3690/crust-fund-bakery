About Crust Fund Bakery

Crust Fund Bakery is a small, independent bakery located near campus, serving fresh-baked pastries, bread, and coffee every day. We started Crust Fund Bakery to give students and neighbors a warm, welcoming spot to grab a quick bite between classes or on the way to work.

Everything on our menu is baked in small batches, and our offerings change weekly — follow us on Instagram to see what's fresh.
